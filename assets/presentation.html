Under the hood

A market that is always open

No order book and no waiting for a counterparty. Each market is a small pool that always quotes a price.

YES shares × NO shares = constant
YES price = NO ÷ (YES + NO)
Winning share = 1 token
⛓️
Why Solana

Fast, low-fee transactions make small trades practical. Anchor programs hold markets, positions and vaults.

👻
Phantom wallet

You sign in your browser; keys never leave the wallet.

🌐
Blazor in the browser

The app runs as WebAssembly and reads data straight from the chain.

What we built

Six instructions, one full cycle

create_market

Anyone opens a YES/NO market with initial liquidity.

buy

Buy shares with slippage protection.

sell

Sell shares for an exact amount of collateral.

resolve_market

The resolver declares the winner after the end time.

redeem

Winners swap shares for collateral 1:1.

claim_pool

The creator collects leftover winning shares.
